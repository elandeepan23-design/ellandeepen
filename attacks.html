Three attacks, three different defences

AttackWhat the attacker starts withWhat stops it
Credential stuffingReal passwords leaked from another siteA unique password per site, plus MFA
Brute forceNothing; it guesses many passwordsA long passphrase, lockout after failures
Password sprayingA short list of very common passwordsBlocking common passwords, lockout

A strong password stops brute force. Only a unique one stops credential stuffing.

Sources: OWASP; vendor explainers on credential stuffing vs brute force vs spraying.

Walk the table row by row. The key line at the bottom: a strong password that was leaked once and reused still fails.
