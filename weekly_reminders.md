# 12-week security-habit reminders (draft)

## Week 1 - Email first

Give your main email a unique 5-6 word passphrase and turn on MFA. Email resets every other account.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 2 - Password manager day

Install a password manager and move 3 accounts into it. Save your master passphrase to memory - not a chat.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 3 - Recovery codes

Print or write your recovery codes and keep them in a locked place, not in WhatsApp or Notes.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 4 - OTP is private

Rule: nobody ever needs your OTP - not a bank, a hostel office, a scholarship desk, or a friend. Hang up and call back on the official number.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 5 - Reuse check

Open your manager's reuse/weak report. Fix the 3 most important reused passwords (email, banking, college portal).
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 6 - Prompt discipline

Got a login approval you didn't start? Tap DENY, then change that account's password.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 7 - Shared devices

Using a lab, cyber-cafe or friend's laptop? Use a private window, never 'remember me', and sign out everywhere after.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 8 - Upgrade your MFA

Move one key account from SMS codes to an authenticator app (or passkey / security key if supported).
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 9 - Account clean-up

Delete 3 old accounts you no longer use. Fewer accounts = fewer breaches to worry about.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 10 - Phone safety

Set a screen-lock PIN and install updates. Your phone is now your second factor.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 11 - Breach check

Check your own email on the official Have I Been Pwned site. If it shows a breach, change that password and anywhere you reused it.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.

## Week 12 - Teach one friend

Show one hostel-mate how to turn on MFA on their email. Habits stick when shared.
Takes 10 minutes. Do it today.
