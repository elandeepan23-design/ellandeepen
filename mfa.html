Climb the MFA ladder

MethodPhishing-resistant?Works offline?Use it for
SMS codeNoNo, needs mobile networkOnly if nothing better exists
Authenticator appNo, a fake site can collect the codeYesMost accounts
Security key or passkeyYes (FIDO2 / WebAuthn)YesEmail and your password-manager vault

Any MFA beats none. Phishing-resistant MFA is the gold standard (CISA).

Sources: CISA; NIST small-business MFA fact sheet (SMS and one-time codes can be phished).

Start where you are: SMS today, authenticator app this month, a passkey or key for email when you can. A passkey or key refuses to work on a look-alike site, which is why CISA calls it phishing-resistant.
