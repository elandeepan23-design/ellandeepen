Your first 30 minutes

1 Install a password manager and set a master passphrase.

2 Give your main email a new passphrase and turn on MFA.

3 Add MFA to your college portal and payment apps.

4 Write down recovery codes and lock them away.

Three rules, always

Never share an OTP with anyone.

Deny any login prompt you did not start.

Keep recovery codes offline, away from your phone.

Practise on dummy accounts only. Never type a real password into a demo or an AI tool.

Close with the action list; each step takes about ten minutes. Then hand out the checklist and run the quiz. Remind the room of the ethics rule: dummy accounts only.
