Password and MFA habits · first-year hostel residents

One leaked password can open every account you reuse it on.

1

A site you used is breached and its logins leak.

2

Bots replay the same email and password on other sites.

3

Your reused password opens your email, then everything else.

This attack is called credential stuffing (OWASP).

Open with the chain, not the jargon. Ask who in the room uses one password on more than one site. Credential stuffing means replaying real leaked logins on other sites; it works because of reuse, not because any password was weak.
